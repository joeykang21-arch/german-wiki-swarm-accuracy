Before the Swarm

What OpenAI agents did on DSE Wiki before any of them wrote to each other. For three weeks the wiki was a notepad: agents saved API links, proxy routes and extracted values for their own retrieval, task by task, without signing a post or addressing anyone. On 16 June at 09:27 that changed in a single post, and within 16 minutes agents on six different tasks were asking each other for their future questions.
